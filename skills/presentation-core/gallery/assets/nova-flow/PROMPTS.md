# NOVA Flow v2 image-generation prompts

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation. Use case:
`photorealistic-natural`. Generated 2026-08-30. The existing NOVA Flow v1 scene
was supplied as a cast and environment reference; the v2 compositions are new.

## Shared photography bible

- Fictional East Asian product team in one restrained warm-gray design studio.
- Diffused northern daylight, quiet concentration, natural skin and fabric,
  subtle 35 mm grain, restrained commercial polish.
- Paper white, charcoal, muted wood, and one small acid-lime environmental cue.
- No logos, readable UI, embedded text, watermark, staged smile, handshake,
  malformed hands, plastic skin, floating interface, or teal-orange grading.
- The sequence must vary behavior, camera height, focal distance, and narrative
  role while preserving cast, location, materials, and light.

## `nova-cover-team-v2.webp`

Create a vertical 4:5-friendly medium-wide documentary cover image of the same
fictional team during a decisive working session. Show three colleagues actively
organizing physical workflow evidence around a black table and laptop. Keep faces
and hand activity clear, provide layered environment, and avoid repeating the
original seated two-person pose.

## `nova-usability-session-v2.webp`

Create a landscape 3:2 usability-observation scene. A participant pauses while
using a laptop in the foreground; the familiar female researcher observes from
the side and writes notes; another team member may remain soft in the background.
The participant action is primary and the observer secondary. Do not repeat card
sorting or two people pointing at one screen.

## `nova-workflow-detail-v2.webp`

Create a landscape 3:2 close overhead-oblique evidence image of two or three
anatomically correct hands resequencing tactile paper wireframe cards after the
session. Include a notebook, laptop edge, and simple timing object; one card uses
a small acid-lime tab and another a muted orange tab. Cards may contain faint
non-readable wireframe marks but no words. Build a diagonal flow from unresolved
cards to one completion signal.
