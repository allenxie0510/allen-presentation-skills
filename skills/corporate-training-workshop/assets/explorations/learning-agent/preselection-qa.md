# Learning Agent pre-selection QA

Status: route comparison, not a formal design-review score. Selection remains
`pending`; no route is user-approved and no complete 15-slide template has been
built.

## Controlled comparison

- Three routes use the same 1920 × 1080 IR, copy, learning model, activity, and
  two-image synthetic documentary set.
- Identity, reasoning, and practice frames were rendered for every route.
- All nine full-size frames and the direction contact sheet were inspected.

## Mechanical gates

- No object leaves the stage.
- No text box reports horizontal or vertical overflow.
- Both embedded images load at full source resolution.
- No visible collision, clipped title, accidental crop, or broken asset remains.
- Synthetic project and synthetic image disclosures are visible.
- Cobalt and lime cues are reinforced by numbering, labels, and position rather
  than being the only carriers of meaning.

## Image realism

- The wide and detail images maintain the same team, practical training room,
  natural wood table, varied low-saturation wardrobe, daylight, cobalt cards,
  and lime marker.
- Wardrobe colors remain individually distinguishable—slate blue, dusty
  terracotta, deep teal, warm taupe/olive, and muted lavender—and no participant
  uses black as the dominant garment.
- Participant gaze and hand activity correspond to a workshop task.
- Hands and fingers are plausible at presentation viewing scale; no duplicate or
  impossible interaction was observed.
- The images contain no readable product UI, company logo, watermark, robot,
  hologram, handshake, staged camera-facing smile, black table, or monochrome
  group wardrobe.
- If a direction is selected, the 15-slide build will require additional
  continuity-controlled images or real project evidence; repeating these two
  images across the full deck is not allowed.

## Route risks to test after selection

- **Learning Blueprint:** may become repetitive if cobalt fields and alternating
  steps are used on too many slides.
- **Workshop Cut:** depends most strongly on continued access to credible human
  and artifact photography.
- **Delegation Protocol:** is the most distinctive, but needs lighter quiet pages
  so an introductory employee audience does not experience it as overly technical.

Formal hard-gate review and five-lens scoring start only after the user selects or
combines a route and representative cover, narrative, data, structure, and ending
frames exist.
