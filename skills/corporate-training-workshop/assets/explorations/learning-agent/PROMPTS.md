# Learning Agent documentary prompt record

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The two images are synthetic
concept-project assets; they do not depict a real company or verified workshop.

## Shared photographic bible

- Same East Asian enterprise team in a practical, visibly used daylight training
  room with an unmarked whiteboard, cork pinboard, modest plant, and ordinary
  office chairs.
- Light-to-medium natural oak or ash table with visible grain and small signs of
  everyday use; never a black conference table.
- Soft natural daylight, warm wood bounce, realistic skin tones, and normal office
  imperfection.
- Every participant wears a different low-saturation office color: slate blue,
  dusty terracotta, deep teal/forest green, warm taupe/olive, and muted lavender.
  No person wears black or near-black as the dominant garment.
- Cobalt stationery is a physical workshop cue; one small acid-lime marker is the
  only secondary accent.
- Editorial documentary treatment, natural gestures, realistic materials, and
  restrained commercial polish.
- No logos, readable interface text, floating UI, robots, neon, handshakes, or
  staged smiles.

## `learning-agent-workshop-wide.webp`

Wide eye-level 35 mm photograph of an AI Agent consultant facilitating four
employees around a natural wood table. Participants discuss printed task cards,
a notebook, and one laptop. The scene emphasizes active learning behavior,
individual wardrobe variation, and credible workplace interaction.

## `learning-agent-practice-detail.webp`

High-oblique documentary view of the same team arranging a four-step delegation
sequence on the same wood table. Hands, simplified non-readable cards, notebook,
laptop edge, and the small lime marker are the evidence. The second generation
used the revised wide image as the continuity reference; a final targeted edit
removed artificial arrow cutouts, bead-like props, and readable card interfaces.

Both production prompts explicitly required anatomically correct hands, plausible
eye lines and overlaps, no readable text, no watermark, and no AI-advertising
effects.
