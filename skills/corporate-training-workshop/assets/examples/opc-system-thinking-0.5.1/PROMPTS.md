# International team image prompt set

Use these prompts as a decision pattern, not as a fixed cast for every project.
Replace the people, roles, culture, workplace, and artifacts from the real brief.

## Cast and place bible

- East Asian woman founder, muted cobalt overshirt and ivory top.
- Black man operations partner, warm taupe knit polo.
- White woman research partner, olive blouse or soft blue knit.
- South Asian man automation partner, soft terracotta overshirt.
- Bright international studio, large windows, pale oak table, off-white acoustic
  wall, mature plants, natural daylight, practical notebooks and laptops.
- Documentary 35 mm language, natural skin and fabric, modest everyday disorder,
  restrained editorial grade, no logos or staged stock-photo smiles.

## Global prompt contract

Generate a photorealistic natural-light workplace photograph for the specified
slide slot. Preserve recurring identities, role agency, office geography,
clothing palette, materials, light, and camera language. Compose for the exact
slot ratio and keep every face, diagram, label, and evidence object inside the
safe zone. Every visible note, card, board, sheet, and screen must support the
slide's system-thinking task. No blank notes, decorative paper, gibberish,
invented UI, logos, all-black wardrobe, dark boardroom, neon, robots, holograms,
or tokenistic diversity.

## Slot prompts

1. `cover · 16:9`: team maps ATTRACT, SELL, DELIVER, RETAIN, LEARN, IMPROVE;
   founder and board in the right half, calm title space on the left.
2. `loop-detail · 16:9`: overhead-oblique team view; six connected loop cards,
   one BOTTLENECK card and one LEVERAGE card; no other paper.
3. `case-review · near 4:5`: founder and partners review a complete loop board;
   SYSTEM REVIEW sheet contains OWNER, CADENCE, FEEDBACK; all board labels and
   faces remain in the central 80% safe zone.
4. `practice · 16:9`: six participants complete SYSTEM MAP and the populated
   OWNER, CADENCE, HANDOFF, FEEDBACK worksheet; no spare sticky notes.
5. `assessment · 16:9`: team reviews SYSTEM TEST evidence under OWNER,
   CYCLE TIME, FEEDBACK; laptop interface remains unreadable and non-evidentiary.
6. `commitment · 16:9`: founder places OWNER under the connected plan 30 DAYS,
   W1 MAP, W2 FIX, W3 RUN, W4 REVIEW; no celebration pose.

## Image QA

Reject or repair an image when:

- a recurring identity, role, clothing family, office, or light changes without
  narrative reason;
- a person is reduced to a diversity token or lacks professional agency;
- a card is blank, decorative, unrelated, duplicated, or contains gibberish;
- a generated screen could be mistaken for real UI or evidence;
- the uncropped source looks correct but the final slide crop clips a face,
  label, diagram, hand, or task artifact;
- hands, eye lines, perspective, paper geometry, or text rendering undermine
  documentary credibility.
