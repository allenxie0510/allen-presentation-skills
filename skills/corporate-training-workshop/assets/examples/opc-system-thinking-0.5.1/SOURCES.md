# Sources and disclosure

## Content

The repaired deck adapts content supplied by the user in an OPC system-thinking
training presentation. Claims are treated as instructional models and concept
examples unless a real project source is supplied. No client outcomes, learner
ratings, or business results are asserted.

## Images

All six PNG files in `photos/` were created with OpenAI image generation for this
concept reference. They depict a fictional East Asian woman founder, a Black man
operations partner, a White woman research partner, a South Asian man automation
partner, and occasional fictional workshop participants in a consistent bright
international studio. They are synthetic and must not be described as real
project documentation.

The visual brief requested natural daylight, an oak worktable, believable office
materials, low-saturation but varied clothing, candid activity, equal professional
agency, and continuity of person, space, light, and camera language. Each image
was composed for its final slide slot; the portrait case image intentionally uses
a tall safe zone. Cards, worksheets, and boards are limited to system loops,
bottleneck, leverage, owner, cadence, feedback, assessment, and transfer content.
The brief excluded blank or decorative notes, unrelated text, gibberish, logos,
readable product interfaces, dramatic black wardrobes, glossy advertising light,
neon effects, and staged corporate posing.

## Typography

The deck uses Noto Sans CJK SC where available with Arial fallback. Noto Sans CJK
is an open-source family; verify font availability and substitution on the target
presentation computer before delivery.
