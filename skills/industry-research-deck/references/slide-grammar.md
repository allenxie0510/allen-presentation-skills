# Industry research slide grammar

| Reasoning job | Preferred visual | Required evidence | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Define the market | boundary diagram with inclusions/exclusions | geography, customer, category, period | undefined TAM circle |
| Size a market | top-down/bottom-up bridge and range | formula, units, source dates, assumptions | one vendor number |
| Show growth | indexed or absolute trend with forecast boundary | historical series and forecast method | unlabeled CAGR arrow |
| Segment demand | mutually exclusive segment matrix or stacked composition | segment definition and total reconciliation | overlapping personas |
| Map value | value-chain flow with economics by stage | participants, transaction/value flows | logo landscape only |
| Compare competitors | criteria-based landscape or benchmark table | comparable measures and periods | arbitrary 2×2 axes |
| Explain structure | force/relationship map with evidence | mechanisms, not just ratings | decorative Five Forces |
| Explain change | driver tree or causal chain | evidence and counterevidence | trend cloud |
| Express uncertainty | scenario matrix with assumptions and indicators | explicit scenario logic | single-point forecast |
| Recommend action | option matrix and strategic implications | choice criteria, risks, triggers | generic opportunities list |

Use source notes on every material analytical slide and a source appendix with
definitions, calculations, limitations, and access dates.
