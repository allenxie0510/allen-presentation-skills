# Image realism QA

Status: pass for concept exploration; not eligible as outcome evidence.

## Cast and world consistency

- The set intentionally uses three distinct learner profiles: an East Asian
  teenager, a European teenager, and a Black North American young adult.
- Clothing uses varied, low-saturation cobalt, terracotta, and sage; every scene
  remains grounded in a real library or community-learning environment.
- Natural daylight, pale wood, books, computers, notebooks, candid gestures, and
  restrained editorial color create one photographic world without implying
  that the three people are a longitudinal cohort.
- The sequence has a semantic progression: define goal → practice → apply and self-review.

## Artifact integrity

- No brand logo or fake product dashboard appears.
- The practice scene uses one small folded-paper prototype and handwritten notes;
  they demonstrate active work without pretending to be an AhaSlope interface.
- The final notebook uses a simple slope diagram rather than invented data.
- Generated notebook micro-writing is treated as non-readable texture and must
  never be enlarged or cited as evidence.

## Slot safety

- Project derivatives are 1920×1080 WebP.
- Every IR image object declares `fit: cover` and a non-default focal position.
- Crops must retain face, both hands, computer, and the learning artifact.
- Do not apply blue or dark overlays that distort skin tone or hide artifacts.

## Rejection triggers

Reject or regenerate if a later crop introduces readable fake UI, decorative
sticky notes, malformed hands, duplicated devices, tokenized diversity, a stock
photo pose, or a scene unrelated to the slide claim.
