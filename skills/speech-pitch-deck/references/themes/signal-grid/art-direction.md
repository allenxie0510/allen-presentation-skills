# Signal Grid 0.5.0 art direction

Status: `provisional`. Selected by the Allen FRAME design lead from three real
rendered directions; not yet user-approved. Comparison board:
`gallery/explorations/nova-flow/previews/direction-board.webp`.

## Concept thesis

Signal Grid makes invisible product friction visible as operational evidence,
then turns that evidence into a resource decision. NOVA Flow is a disclosed
synthetic concept project used to prove the theme, not a customer case study.

## Selected direction

**Operational Documentary / 运营纪实** is the structural base. It performed best
across human context, analytical pause, and decision clarity. Editorial Evidence
was legible but emotionally distant. Signal Cartography's continuous rail became
too decorative, so only semantic indexing and action marks survive.

## Visual system

- Composition: large asymmetric documentary field paired with a compact written
  decision field; evidence pages may remove imagery to create an analytical pause.
- Typography: embedded Smiley Sans for short Chinese assertions, neutral sans for
  evidence and captions. Display type is expressive; body type is not.
- Image cadence: context / analytical pause / two evidence strips (`1 / 0 / 2`).
- Image roles: team alignment, user observation, physical workflow evidence.
  The same action or camera angle cannot appear twice in one short sequence.
- Signal motif: acid lime identifies a decision, completion signal, or next action.
  It is never a decorative background color. Page numbers and evidence labels are
  small operational coordinates, not a fake technical interface.
- Surfaces: cool paper gray, white evidence modules, matte black type, no generic
  gradients, glass panels, glow, or ornamental dashboards.

## Crop and alignment

- Cover evidence field: `1110 × 920` at `(64, 96)`; authored crop `center 52%`.
- Closing evidence strips share `y=772`, `h=244`, and a 24 px gap. They may have
  different widths because one establishes behavior and one shows close evidence.
- Captions sit 32 px inside the image edge and state the evidence interpretation.
- Inspect faces, hands, cards, laptop edges, and caption collisions at full size.

## Cadence

1. **Context peak** — documentary field dominates; title remains a separate
   authored object rather than text over a face.
2. **Analytical pause** — no photography; metrics and interpretation carry the
   reasoning with generous separation.
3. **Decision convergence** — a large verbal decision followed by two aligned
   evidence strips and a named next-evidence requirement.

## Anti-patterns

- repeated two-person laptop poses;
- a lime rail with no semantic destination;
- captions that merely describe objects;
- synthetic participants presented as customers;
- tiny evidence text used to rescue an overloaded slide;
- equal card grids when the underlying evidence is unequal.
