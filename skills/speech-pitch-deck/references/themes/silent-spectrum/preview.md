# Silent Spectrum · 静谱 — 0.3.0

Status: `approved` / 0.3.0. This cross-scenario theme combines Spectral Pulse scale
cadence with Silent Index alignment discipline. It adds stronger Chinese display
type, more generous leading, semantic English and numeric composition, and
scenario-specific line illustrations. Open-source Source Han Sans, Source Han
Serif, and Smiley Sans web fonts are bundled for self-contained rendering.

![Five scenario representative passes](../../gallery/explorations/silent-spectrum-0.1.0/family-previews/family-board.webp)

Read `art-direction.md` before using the theme. Five complete scenario IRs live
under `gallery/ir/*--silent-spectrum.yaml`; each includes a full narrative path,
concept disclosure, at least two high-density evidence or decision frames, and
speaker/facilitator notes where the scenario requires them.
