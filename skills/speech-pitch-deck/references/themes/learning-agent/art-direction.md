# Learning Agent 0.4.0 art-direction contract

## Concept

把 Agent 从抽象能力转译为员工可以练习、委托并验收的工作协议。视觉主体是
学习行为和工作物证，而不是科技氛围。

Selected FRAME route: **Workshop Cut / 现场切片**. Learning Blueprint and
Delegation Protocol remain documented alternatives, not released themes.

## Composition and rhythm

- Use asymmetric documentary-image fields, solid cobalt teaching fields, and
  quiet white explanation pages instead of one repeated split layout.
- Alternate quiet, working, and peak frames. Never place two photo-led peak
  frames back to back unless the narrative requires a continuous scene.
- Preserve an 80 px minimum authored edge and align major content to a common
  80/112 px system. Use blue rules for orientation, not decoration.
- Titles make one claim and use deliberate line breaks. Body text remains wider,
  calmer, and more open than the compressed title voice.

## Typography

- Chinese: Noto Sans CJK SC, Noto Sans SC, Source Han Sans SC, then system
  Chinese sans-serif fallbacks. These are free/open-source or system fallbacks.
- Latin labels: the same sans family in compact uppercase with generous tracking.
- Titles use heavy weight, tight tracking, and 1.04–1.12 line height. Instructions
  use 1.35–1.5 line height and never inherit the compressed title treatment.

## Image system

- Images must show a learning action, artifact, decision, or transfer moment.
- Use one continuity-controlled team, room, daylight, material, and wardrobe
  language per project. Real project photography overrides synthetic examples.
- Prefer 16:9 or 4:3 documentary frames, natural eye level or purposeful high
  oblique detail. Record `fit`, `position`, caption, source, and disclosure.
- Wardrobe may be low-saturation but must remain individually varied. Avoid black
  tables, monochrome groups, staged smiles, handshakes, robots, holograms,
  floating UI, and unreadable faux interfaces.
- Generated images are disclosed as synthetic and must pass anatomy, continuity,
  crop, source, and relevance QA.

## Components and evidence

- Cobalt establishes chapter, model, or activity orientation.
- Acid lime is reserved for current learning action, human review, or completion.
- Process stages, decision tests, activity briefs, assessment criteria, and
  transfer commitments are reusable component families.
- No number, result, learner quote, testimonial, or business impact may be
  invented to improve a composition.

## Scenario adaptation

For non-Agent training, replace the four-step delegation motif with a
content-born model while preserving documentary evidence, instructional
hierarchy, activity clarity, and the quiet/working/peak cadence. If the visual
metaphor cannot transfer without repeated page-specific exceptions, select a
different theme rather than imitating Learning Agent surfaces.
