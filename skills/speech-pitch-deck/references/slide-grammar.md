# Speech and pitch slide grammar

| Reasoning job | Preferred visual | Required evidence | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook attention | one short assertion, image, or concrete scene | relevance to core idea | agenda slide |
| State the idea | single-sentence statement | defensible meaning | slogan without argument |
| Establish stakes | contrast, before/after, or human-scale example | context and magnitude | abstract urgency words |
| Explain a mechanism | progressive causal chain or simple diagram | supported relationships | complex systems map at once |
| Demonstrate | live demo frame, sequence, or annotated artifact | tested setup and fallback | fake product UI |
| Prove traction | one directly labeled trend or milestone sequence | source, period, definition | metric wall |
| Size opportunity | simple range or bottom-up build | definitions and assumptions | giant unsourced TAM circle |
| Differentiate | criteria-based comparison | fair comparable criteria | logo graveyard |
| Humanize | contextual image or authorized quote | permission and context | generic stock emotion |
| Ask | explicit action, amount, owner, and next step | rationale and use | “thank you” only |
| Close | callback to opening idea or future state | narrative continuity | new evidence at the end |

Use speaker notes for narration, sources, cues, and transitions. Keep the stage
view concise enough to understand while listening.
