# Aster Yard image realism QA

Review date: 2026-08-31. Status: passed for a disclosed synthetic direction
study. This report does not prove that the depicted project or research occurred.

## World continuity

- Pale mineral surfaces, ash wood, brushed metal, muted moss, warm daylight, and
  restrained orange repeat across context and application images.
- Clothing is varied and low-saturation rather than uniformly black.
- People act in plausible customer, design, and participant roles without staged
  handshakes or direct-to-camera stock poses.

## Anatomy and artifacts

- Visible faces and hands were inspected at source size; no fused or duplicated
  digits were accepted.
- Research cards contain structured diagrams without audience-facing fake
  findings or readable nonsense paragraphs.
- Application artifacts use a generic geometric concept mark and contain no
  brand name, slogan, watermark, fake interface, or copied user-reference mark.

## Slot and crop QA

- Every image source exceeds 1000 × 800 and includes descriptive alternative
  text.
- Every slot records `fit`, explicit `position`, narrative role, disclosure, and
  a minimum source-size constraint.
- All nine route-specific image treatments were inspected at full 1920 × 1080
  scale. Faces, active hands, the research card field, and the application mark
  remain within their authored crops.

## Narrative boundary

- Context image: establishes the type of place and user mix.
- Research image: establishes a synthetic research setting, not a finding.
- Application image: tests the identity and touchpoint system, not a shipped
  implementation.
