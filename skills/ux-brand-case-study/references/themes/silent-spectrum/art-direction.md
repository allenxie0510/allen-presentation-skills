# Silent Spectrum 0.3.0 art-direction contract

## Concept

Silent Spectrum makes the changing density of a presentation visible. Sparse
assertions, structured evidence, timelines, and decisions occupy different
positions on one quiet spectrum. The system is not generic minimalism: line
density, type scale, and tonal weight must correspond to an information job.

Allen Xie selected `spectral-pulse` as the primary direction and explicitly
mixed in the strict alignment discipline of `silent-index`. The selection is
user-approved. Version 0.3.0 completes and validates the five full scenario
templates for self-contained web rendering.

## Foundation

- Canvas: fixed 1920 × 1080, twelve-column logic, 72–100 px outer margins.
- Primary ink: `#121416`; supporting charcoal: `#484E50`.
- Fields: warm, mineral, sage, and blue-gray. No pure-white card wall.
- Light surfaces never use white or near-white text. All audience text must pass
  the surface-aware 4.5:1 contrast audit.
- Rules are 1 px and semantic. Every line must terminate at a label, node,
  threshold, source, or aligned component edge.
- Corners stay square or nearly square; no glass, floating shadow, glow, or
  decorative gradient.

## Typography and cadence

- Chinese body text uses bundled open-source Source Han Sans. Product,
  industry, speech, UX/brand, and training titles may choose different members
  of the same open-source family strategy: a heavy sans for operational work, a
  bundled Source Han Serif for research and case-study judgment, and bundled
  Smiley Sans for live-stage emphasis. Fall back to the shared body family when
  a title face is unavailable.
- Multi-line display titles use visibly more leading than the first exploration:
  generally 1.06–1.18 depending on the face and room size. Do not compress
  Chinese lines merely to imitate a Latin reference.
- Extreme display size is reserved for a cover, one decisive number, one section
  index, or the closing decision—not every page.
- English words, large numbers, section indices, and units may operate as both
  meaning and composition. They must remain traceable to the content and may not
  become empty pseudo-technical decoration.
- Use three energies: quiet 25–35% occupancy, working 45–60%, peak 65–75% with a
  single dominant object. Never keep all pages equally full.
- Supporting text stays dark gray and materially smaller than assertions, but
  not below room-readable limits.

## Scenario expressions

- Product Roadmap: vertically displaced outcome signals, explicit priority and
  decision rules; no feature calendar.
- Industry Research: visible scope and source rail, thin range bars, restrained
  evidence density; no unsourced market-size spectacle.
- Speech Pitch: the strongest type-scale contrast and the sparsest proof page;
  no script paragraphs or buried ask.
- UX Brand Case: editorial serif option, fair before/after fields, annotated
  artifacts when real assets exist; no beauty gallery.
- Corporate Training: indexed practice sequence, clear timebox and transfer
  commitment; no passive topic agenda.

## Vector and image behavior

- Prefer vectors when sequence, range, dependency, hierarchy, or system logic is
  the actual evidence. The shared pack includes five scenario-specific line
  families: converging roadmap paths, research ranges, speech pulses, UX system
  relations, and training loops.
- Photography is optional, never filler. When used, it occupies one deliberate
  field and receives an evidence caption, crop rule, provenance, and disclosure.
- Do not copy any user reference image, brand mark, UI, icon family, or exact
  composition.

## Release boundary

Version 0.3.0 is approved for the bundled self-contained web runtime. The five
scenario templates total 48 slides and include full narrative paths, dense
evidence stress frames, open-source font packaging, concept disclosure,
surface-aware contrast checks, geometry checks, contact-sheet review, and
full-size inspection. Native editable PPTX remains a separate projection and
requires a compatible renderer; this release does not claim one is bundled.
