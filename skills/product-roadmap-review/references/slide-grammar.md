# Product roadmap slide grammar

| Reasoning job | Preferred visual | Required evidence | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome health | outcome scorecard with baseline, target, actual, confidence | metric definition, cohort, period | unlabeled KPI cards |
| Progress over time | annotated trend with interventions | consistent series and event dates | two-point growth claims |
| Outcome → opportunity → solution | opportunity solution tree | research-backed opportunity nodes | invented branches or feature tree |
| Commitment under uncertainty | Now / Next / Later roadmap | commitment and confidence rules | detailed dates for unvalidated work |
| Prioritization | criteria-by-option matrix or weighted comparison | criteria, weights, sensitivity | unexplained rank list |
| Trade-off | strategic choices matrix or paired consequences | benefit, cost, displaced work | “everything is priority” grid |
| Dependency | dependency map or critical path | owner, dependency type, timing | decorative process arrows |
| Capacity | allocation bars or scenario comparison | capacity assumptions and protected work | percentage totals above 100% |
| Risk | likelihood-impact register with trigger and mitigation | owner and leading signal | generic red/amber/green dots |
| Leadership decision | decision card with options and recommendation | decision owner, deadline, consequence | vague “discussion” ending |

Sequence evidence before choice. Use an appendix for backlog detail, scoring
math, research notes, or delivery-level plans that would interrupt the decision
story.
